Department Introduction

Launching:2022

The Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science is committed to delivering a future-ready curriculum that blends theory, coding, analytics, and real-world problem solving.

Our first batch marks the beginning of an inspiring journey of innovation, collaboration, and leadership in emerging technologies.

Vision

  • To become a centre of excellence in the realms of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science which fosters talents, cultivates research skills and promotes social responsibility.

Mission

  • Foster employability and entrepreneurial skills in AI & DS through experiential and self-directed learning.
  • Strive towards efficient industry institute interaction to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry needs.
  • Sensitize students to solve societal issues through AI techniques while inculcating values and ethics.
  • Train students in the state-of-the-art technologies of AI & DS.

    Our Student project

    PREDICTIVE ANALYTIC MODEL FOR IDENTIFYING BUSINESS LOSS IN SMALL SMALL SCALE INDUSTRIES

    Team members - Jeevan.J, Pandeshwari , Gowtham , Arul prasath

    Aim of the Project:

    The aim of this project is to develop an AI-based predictive analytics model that identifies potential business losses in small-scale industries by analyzing historical business data. The system provides early risk predictions and insights to help business owners make informed decisions, reduce financial losses, and improve overall business performance and sustainability.

    Project Title : SMARTSAFE AI

    Collaboration with Namakkal SP office

    Teammates : A. Mohamed salman, M. Harishkumar, J. Jeevan, D.Gokulraja

    Aim of the Project:

    The aim of SMARTSAFE AI is to enhance road safety by using artificial intelligence to detect unsafe driving behavior, prevent accidents through real-time warnings, and provide immediate emergency assistance after a collision. The project seeks to reduce road fatalities, improve driver awareness, and create safer roads for everyone.

    Workshop

    Month:August

    Venue: CMSCE

    A workshop is a hands-on, interactive training session where participants actively learn practical skills, solve problems, or collaborate on projects. Unlike a passive lecture, it emphasizes peer interaction, physical tools, and real-time application of knowledge

    Hackathon

    Month:September

    Venue: CMSCE

    A hackathon is an intensive, fast-paced event where programmers, designers, and innovators collaborate in teams to build functioning software or hardware prototypes under a strict time limit (usually 24 to 48 hours). It emphasizes rapid problem-solving, creative engineering, and high-energy teamwork to solve real-world challenges.

    Faculty Development Program(FDP)

    Month: October

    Venue:CMSCE

    A Faculty Development Program (FDP) is an intensive training initiative designed to upgrade the teaching, research, and technical skills of educators. It introduces professors to modern pedagogies, emerging digital tools, and advanced industry practices to improve classroom learning outcomes.

    Guest Lecture

    Month: November

    Venue: CMSCE

    A guest lecture is an academic session where an external industry expert, researcher, or professional is invited to share real-world insights, specialized knowledge, and current trends with students. It bridges the gap between textbook theory and practical field applications while expanding students' professional networks

    Internal conference

    Month: December

    Venue: CMSCE

    An internal conference is an institutional or corporate event where researchers, faculty, or employees within the same organization gather to present their work, share findings, and foster cross-departmental collaboration.
